Pooled confusion matrices for the 16 model x strategy configurations. Rows give the expert label and columns the model prediction (positive class: disclosed); TN, FP, FN and TP are exact counts of policy x criterion decisions pooled over the five repetitions of the policies successfully evaluated in each configuration (policy coverage and decision totals are shown above each panel). Section strategies use the OR (≥1 section) rule across sections. Failed records are excluded, not scored as zero. The pooled cells are repeated predictions on the same policies, and criteria within a policy may be related, so they are descriptive and not independent observations. Percentages are the share of decisions with that expert label (TN%: specificity; TP%: recall).
